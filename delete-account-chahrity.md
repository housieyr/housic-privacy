# Delete your Chahrity account and data

You can delete your Chahrity account and the related data directly in the app.

## How to delete your account

1. Open **Chahrity** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Type the confirmation word shown by the app and confirm deletion.

## Data deleted

When deletion succeeds, Chahrity deletes:

- Your Firebase account.
- Your financial data stored in Firebase, including income, expenses, balances, commitments, plans, goals, and custom categories.
- Feedback and suggestions saved under your account.
- The local copy of your data on the device.

The deletion is completed immediately after confirmation. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data. Reports you previously shared outside the app cannot be deleted by Chahrity.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details listed on Google Play.
