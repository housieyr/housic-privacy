# Delete Chahrity data without deleting your account

Chahrity lets you delete individual financial records while keeping your account.

## How to delete a financial record

1. Open **Chahrity** and sign in.
2. Open **Transaction history**.
3. Select the transaction you want to remove.
4. Choose **Delete** and confirm.

The deleted transaction is removed from the local copy and from your Firebase data after synchronization. Your Chahrity account and other financial records remain available.

To delete your entire account and all related data, visit the [Chahrity account deletion page](https://github.com/housieyr/housic-privacy/blob/main/delete-account-chahrity.md).

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details listed on Google Play.
